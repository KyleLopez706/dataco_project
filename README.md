# DataCo Global Supply Chain Delivery Risk Dashboard
**Foundations of Business Analytics (FOBA) Final Project**  
**Group 10:** Keann Geo Aguinaldo & Dreizen Kyle Lopez  

## Overview
This repository hosts the interactive, zero-leakage **Descriptive & Predictive Analytics Dashboard** for the DataCo Smart Supply Chain Dataset (180,519 customer orders, $36.78M in gross sales).

### Key Features:
- **5 Headline KPIs:** Total Orders (180,519), On-Time Rate (45.17%), Late Delivery Risk Rate (54.83%), At-Risk Gross Sales ($20.13M), and Average SLA Gap (+0.57 Days).
- **6 Core Visualizations:** Each answering a specific business question with dedicated takeaway notes.
- **4 Interactive Global Slicers:** Shipping Mode, Geographic Market, Customer Segment, and Order Year.
- **Advanced Predictive ML Simulator:** Powered by a champion `HistGradientBoosting` classifier (72.41% Test Accuracy, 85.77% Precision, 0.8135 ROC-AUC) to screen late delivery risk before package dispatch.
